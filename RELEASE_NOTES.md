CardMirror Green 1.16.14 — Collaboration in one place

Open Collaboration beside Send and Receive, on Home, or in the tablet Menu. The docked panel brings saved partners, shared documents and invitations together. Pick a default partner, then use Co-edit with their name for the active document. Invitations remain pending until accepted; live partner presence and changes waiting to send are shown separately.

The panel keeps the document clear, shows the host's Prep/Round sharing rule, and offers a verified local recovery copy before disconnecting. Recovery writes now retry after storage failures. Original manual codes, links, group invitations, spectators and card transfers remain available.

This release also includes the recent document-to-Flow/XLSX helpers and extraction improvements from the other development branch. The batch schema accepts the new optional table references while retaining compatibility with older exports.

On Windows or an Apple Silicon Mac, use Help > Check for Updates, save your work, then Restart to install.
