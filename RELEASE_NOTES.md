CardMirror Green 1.16.9 — Faster Flow row creation

Pressing Enter or Down at the bottom of a Flow now adds the next row without rebuilding the entire spreadsheet. Focus moves directly into the new cell, including in the Prep column. This improves existing Flows as well as new ones.

Use Help > Check for Updates, download 1.16.9, then choose Restart to install. Mac builds remain ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
