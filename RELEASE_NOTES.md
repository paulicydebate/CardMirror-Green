CardMirror Green 1.16.10 — Clearer Prep / Round and partner collaboration

On Windows and Mac, use the Prep / Round control in the bottom status pane. Start Round now shows a checklist naming the protected documents, documents that will close, file restrictions and collaboration rules. Readable typing logs show timestamps, typing, blocked paste attempts and partner edits separately. Unconfirmed input is not treated as proof of cheating.

Co-edit with… beside Send / Receive lists your saved partners and names the active document. Choose a partner to start or reuse that document's live session and send its invitation automatically. Your partner chooses Join in Receive. Existing card sending, group, code, link and view-only workflows remain available.

Full Save As now retains a sealed prep document's identity after End Session, so its certificate remains checkable. Save sealed documents as CardMirror (.cmir); Word copies do not retain the certificate.

The Green iPad web app also supports Prep / Round, readable log exports and the named-partner workflow. Browser restrictions cover CardMirror only and cannot monitor other apps or devices. Export local logs and have the judge record the final seal independently.

On Windows or an Apple Silicon Mac, use Help > Check for Updates, download 1.16.10, then choose Restart to install after saving your work. Both collaborators should update. Mac builds remain ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
